Tips & tricks

Short, practical rules that actually change your life. No unnecessary talk.

Fitness

Simple fat loss rules

  • Daily: 8k–12k steps + water + sleep.
  • Protein first: every meal has protein.
  • Train 4–6 days/week. Consistency beats perfect workouts.
  • Track weight weekly, not daily panic.
Lifestyle

Upgrade mindset

  • Make a “minimum day” plan: even on bad days, you do something.
  • Remove distractions: focus is a skill.
  • Document your life: photos + short notes become legacy.
  • Growth = tiny changes repeated for months.
