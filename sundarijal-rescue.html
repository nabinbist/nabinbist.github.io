INCIDENT REPORT

Sundarijal Rescue

A day that started like a normal trip, then turned into a moment I will never forget.

Quick overview

I went to Sundarijal with my friends Adit and Sunil. We were there to explore, take photos, and enjoy the river. A group of nursing students were also nearby, taking pictures on rocks in the middle of the water.

Location: Sundarijal
Type: River rescue
Outcome: Safe evacuation

What happened

While I was a little away from my friends, I heard screaming. Two girls slipped into the river. One drifted closer to the bank, and I pulled her to safety.

The second girl was in a dangerous spot. The water was deep and moving hard. She was holding onto a rock and her head kept going under at times. Everyone around was crying and calling for help.

Rescue and evacuation

I moved fast across the rocks. The landing hurt my foot, but there was no time to stop. I got close to her and told her to stay calm and trust me. I held her hand and pulled with everything I had until she was out of the water.

After that, the group was still stuck on the rock and too afraid to move. Adit arrived and helped from the riverbank. Together, we guided them one by one until everyone was safely evacuated.

Afterwards

The girl I saved thanked me and said she felt like she got a second life. Her friends were shaking, crying, and hugging each other. When the moment finally settled, the only thing I felt was relief and gratitude.

That day reminded me that a random decision can become a serious responsibility. If I had stayed frozen for even a few seconds, the outcome could have been different. I still feel proud of what we did.

Lessons

Rivers look calm until they are not. Rocks are slippery. A photo is never worth a life. If people are standing on unstable rocks, it only takes one small mistake to turn it into an emergency.
