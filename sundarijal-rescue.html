Sundarijal
14th Sep 2017

Sundarijal Rescue

This is a real day from my life. It happened fast. It was loud and chaotic. I remember the water, the rocks, and the moment people froze.

Location: Sundarijal
Type: River rescue
Note: Photo taken before the incident
Story

On 14th September 2017, I went to Sundarijal with my friends Adit and Sunil. We planned to explore, take photos, and enjoy the river area. A group of nursing students were also there taking pictures on the rocks.

Everything looked calm. Then it changed in seconds.

Timeline
1
The first fall
Start

I was a little away from my friends when I heard screaming. Two girls slipped from the rocks and fell into the river.

2
First rescue
Fast

One girl drifted closer to the riverbank. I moved quickly and pulled her out. She was shaking, but she was safe.

3
Critical moment
Danger

The second girl was in real danger. The current was strong and she was stuck near a rock. Her head kept going under. People were panicking and crying.

4
I moved
Action

There was no time to think too much. I jumped across the rocks. I hurt my foot while landing, but I did not stop.

5
The rescue
Hold

I reached her, told her to stay calm, and grabbed her hand firmly. The water pushed hard, but I did not let go. After a few intense seconds, I pulled her out onto the rock.

6
Getting everyone out
Team

Others were still stranded and afraid to move. Adit reached the riverbank and helped guide them. We got them across one by one until everyone was safe.

7
After it ended
Quiet

When things finally calmed down, it hit me. If I had hesitated, the outcome could have been very different. That day taught me that calm water can still be dangerous.

Archive
What I learned

Rivers look peaceful until they are not. Wet rocks are not a joke. Currents do not care if you are ready.

That day also showed me something personal. When a moment demands action, you either move or you regret it. I chose to move.
